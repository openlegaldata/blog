Open Legal Data

Legal Research

Open Legal Data started as a research project and is built for research: the court decisions, laws and citations are available as open datasets, through a REST API and through an MCP server for AI assistants. Our approach is described in Towards an Open Platform for Legal Information (JCDL 2020, arXiv).

Since then, Open Legal Data has been cited in 65 papers: it is part of the training data of open language models, the basis of new legal datasets and benchmarks, and used in work on legal search, question answering, citation analysis and legal NLP.
