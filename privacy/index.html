Web analytics with Matomo

We use the open-source web analytics software Matomo, which we run ourselves at matomo.openlegaldata.io on webspace of ALL-INKL.COM – Neue Medien Münnich (Friedersdorf, Germany), who processes the data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR). No data is passed on to the vendor or other third parties. The tracker is configured not to store or read any information on your device: it sets no cookies, uses no local storage and does not read device properties such as screen resolution or browser plugins. It only sends what your browser transmits with every page view anyway (address of the page, referrer, browser and operating system from the user agent, language), the page title and the time of the visit, and, for clicks on external links or downloads, the target address.

Your IP address is shortened by two bytes as soon as it is received; the full address is not stored. Page views of one visit are grouped with a checksum whose salt changes daily, so you cannot be recognised on later days or across websites. Visit data is deleted after 90 days; only aggregated, anonymous reports are kept. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time (Art. 21 GDPR): if “Do Not Track” is enabled in your browser, the tracker sends no data at all. You can also block the script from matomo.openlegaldata.io.
